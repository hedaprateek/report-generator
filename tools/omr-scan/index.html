1 · Sheet definition

Open the OMR Designer, build your sheet, then use Download scan file. Without it the scanner has no idea where the bubbles are — this is what makes reading reliable rather than guesswork.

2 · Answer key

Set the correct option for each question. Leave any question blank and it is simply excluded from the score rather than counted wrong.

3 · Scan filled sheets

Lay the sheet flat, get all four corner squares in frame, and avoid heavy shadow. Several images at once is fine.
